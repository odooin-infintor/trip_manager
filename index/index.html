Trip Management for Odoo

The Trip Management module is designed to help tour agents create dynamic, customized itineraries quickly and efficiently. It streamlines the entire trip planning process by enabling agents to build, modify, and organize travel plans with ease, reducing manual effort and improving productivity. The system allows agents to build multiple package options per enquiry — Standard, Deluxe, Premium and Luxury — and compare them based on cost, ensuring they can recommend the most suitable options for their customers while maintaining budget requirements. By providing flexible itinerary creation, real-time cost calculations, and side-by-side package comparisons, the module helps agents deliver personalized travel experiences faster and with greater accuracy. Overall, Trip Management simplifies itinerary planning, optimizes package selection, and enhances operational efficiency, enabling tour agencies to provide better customer service and increase sales.

Key Features of Trip Management for Odoo

Tour Enquiry Management

Capture and manage every tour enquiry in one place. Each enquiry gets an auto-generated reference number and tracks customer details, travel dates, destinations, and requirements — from first contact through to a confirmed booking.

Multi-Option Package Comparison

Build several package options for a single enquiry — Standard, Deluxe, Premium and Luxury — each with its own itinerary, hotels and pricing. Compare them side by side and recommend the best fit for your customer's budget.


Dynamic Day-Wise Itinerary Builder

Create detailed, day-by-day itineraries with automatic day numbering. Assign hotels, activities, entry tickets and guides to each day, and reorganize plans in seconds as customer requirements change.

Hotel & Room Booking

Manage hotel bookings with configurable room types, room rates and extra-bed pricing. Hotels can be filtered and ordered by preferred star rating, so agents always see the most relevant stays first.

Add-ons & Inclusions

Attach inclusions such as Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Entry Tickets and Guides to any day of the trip. Entry tickets sync automatically to the right itinerary day, keeping inclusions accurate without manual re-entry.

Transportation & Flights

Plan ground transportation and vehicles alongside dedicated flight lines for each package option. All travel legs feed straight into the cost calculation and the final quotation.


Real-Time Cost Calculation

Hotels, add-ons, transport and flights roll up into live per-option totals as you build. Agents always know where each package stands on price, with no spreadsheets or manual maths.

Professional Quotation PDF

Generate a polished, print-ready quotation for your customer at the click of a button. The report presents each package option with a clean pricing breakdown, showing flight charges only when they apply.
